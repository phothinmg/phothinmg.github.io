## Comment Policy

Welcome! This is a personal blog focused on sharing clean code, tutorials, and development insights. To keep discussions high-quality, constructive, and safe for everyone, all comments must follow these guidelines:

- **Strict Pre-Moderation**: Every comment is reviewed manually before it appears on the site. Thank you for your patience.
- **No Guest Accounts**: You must log in with a verified Disqus or social media account to post.
- Code Formatting: Please use clear formatting or markdown when sharing code snippets to keep threads readable.
- Be Civil: No personal attacks, insults, or aggressive behavior toward the author or other developers.
- No Spam or Self-Promotion: Link-dropping, unsolicited advertising, and off-topic promotional comments will be permanently deleted.
- Stay On-Topic: Keep your questions and insights relevant to the specific blog post or programming concept being discussed.

Failure to follow these rules will result in your comment being rejected and potentially a permanent ban from commenting.
