## Recommended Breakpoints for 330x330

- **330w (1x)**: For standard low-DPI desktop monitors.
- **495w (1.5x)**: Specifically for the Moto G and similar mobile devices often used by Lighthouse for testing.
- **660w (2x)**: For most modern smartphones and "Retina" laptops.
- **990w (3x)**: For high-end devices like the iPhone Pro or Pixel series.
